mindful-backup.timer (systemd/user, nightly 03:00) runs bin/mindful-backup.mjs and writes whole-store tar.gz + .sha256 to ~/d/mindful/archive/backup/v6. It works (4 s per run) but never prunes: as of 2026-09-23 there are 11 nightly archives at ~31 MB each, ~1 GB/month growth in Dropbox. Requested (mind6 session 2026-09-23): weekly backups stored in ~/d/mindful/backups. Plan: point --output (unit + attachments.conf drop-in) at ~/d/mindful/backups, keep the nightly run, and add retention to the script: keep the last 7 nightlies plus the newest archive of each ISO week for 12 weeks (~600 MB steady state); prune only archives the script itself names (mindful-<iso>-<uuid>.tar.gz + .sha256), never the cutover/rehearsal dirs or the v3 dumps already in ~/d/mindful/backups/archive. Rejected: switching the timer to weekly only (loses up to 6 days of edits for no real saving). Decide whether to move the existing nightlies or leave them in the old dir. Done when a run writes to the new dir and prunes to the policy, with a dry-run test of the retention selection.
